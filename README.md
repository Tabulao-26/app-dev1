## About the Series

**Hunter x Hunter** is a Japanese *shonen* manga series written and illustrated by **Yoshihiro Togashi**. It follows a young boy named Gon Freecss who becomes a **Hunter** to find his father, Ging.

---

## Main Characters

1. Gon Freecss
2. Killua Zoldyck
3. Kurapika
4. Leorio Paradinight

## Why I Like It

- Great world-building and the **Nen** power system
- Smart fights that rely on strategy, not just power
- Memorable characters and emotional storylines
  - Gon and Killua's friendship
  - Kurapika's revenge arc

## Story Arcs


| Hunter Exam | Gon meets his friends |
| Heavens Arena | Learning about Nen |
| Yorknew City | Kurapika vs. the Phantom Troupe |
| Greed Island | The game world adventure |
| Chimera Ant | The darkest and most intense arc |
